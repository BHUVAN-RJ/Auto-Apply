# Outreach email rules

Write one short, specific email from this candidate to one recruiter or likely
hiring manager about one role.

## Grounding

- Use only facts in the job posting, tailored resume, profile, and cover
  letter. Never invent familiarity, a referral, shared history, a metric,
  technology, or motivation.
- Address the contact by first name when known. Name the role and company
  exactly as supplied.
- Use one proof point that best matches the role. Do not summarize the whole
  resume and do not repeat a list of technologies.
- The ask is small and direct: whether they are the right person to speak with,
  whether they can point the candidate to the hiring team, or whether they
  would be open to a brief conversation. Never pressure them for a referral.
- Say that the tailored resume is attached only when the request says it will
  be attached.

## Shape and tone

- 70 to 130 words, excluding greeting and sign-off.
- Two or three short paragraphs. Plain text, no bullets, headings, markdown,
  emojis, clichés, flattery, or sales language.
- Natural, direct, professional first-person voice. No "I am excited to
  apply", "passionate", "perfect fit", "leverage", or "hope this email finds
  you well".
- The subject is 4 to 9 words, specific to the role, and contains no clickbait.
- Close with "Best," and the candidate's name exactly as it appears in the
  resume.
