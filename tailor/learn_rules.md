# Learning from feedback

Below are the change requests the person made on their tailored
applications: each one asked the assistant to redo a resume or a cover
letter differently, for one job. Your job is to find what they want on
*every* application, and turn that into prompt edits, so they stop having
to ask.

## What counts

- A preference stated on two or more different jobs ("shorter summary" on
  one, "the summary is too long" on another) is a standing preference.
- A single request worded as a standing rule ("never", "always", "I don't
  like", "stop") is one too.
- Anything tied to one posting ("mention Kafka for this one", "they want
  Go") is not. Leave it.
- A request already covered by a rule in the prompts is not new. Leave it,
  unless the requests show the rule is not being followed; then make the
  rule more specific.

When nothing qualifies, say so in one sentence and make no edit. Fewer,
surer edits are better than many guesses: the person reviews every one.

## Reply

First, one or two sentences to the person saying what you learned, each
point with the job requests it came from, quoted briefly. Then the edit
blocks, in the same format and under the same rules as the Workshop
instructions that follow.
