Software Engineer, New Grad

We are looking for engineers at the start of their career to join the platform team. You will work on the services that carry our traffic, on the tools the rest of engineering builds against, and on whatever is in the way that week. We care more about how you think than about the list of languages you have used, and we will teach you the rest.

What you will do

  • Build and run services that other teams depend on.
  • Read a great deal of code that somebody else wrote.
  • Write the thing down before you write the thing.

What we are looking for

  • A degree, or the equivalent of one earned somewhere else.
  • Some experience of a real system with real users on it.
